\paragraph{Descriptive external budget accounting.}
Using only the saved predictions for the same two policies and primary window, we apply equation~\eqref{eq:budget} after the confirmatory result (Table~\ref{tab:externalbudget}). The common set has WAPE 0.5899 and supplies 8,421.10 units of slack. Row-only acceptance adds 10,557 rows but just 3.32\% of total demand, with WAPE 1.0046 and mean forecast 0.122; it consumes 2,542.83 units (30.20\%) of that slack. Mixed-only acceptance adds 5,855 rows and 8.48\% of demand, consuming 2,480.43 units (29.45\%). Thus similar excess expenditure supports very different row and demand composition. Both pooled sets retain slack; their failed simultaneous operating checks are unchanged. This retrospective accounting connects the transferred policy contrast to the budget mechanism, without asserting policy optimality or adding a confirmatory endpoint.
