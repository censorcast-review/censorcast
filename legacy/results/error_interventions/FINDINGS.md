# Findings

The two prespecified interventions did not repair Hobbies feasibility. Using the 107,384 Hobbies rows from the original first-seed 600,000-row training sample, we fitted a specialist on the original 21 features and a second specialist adding eight 56/112-day observed-history intermittency summaries. Both used 220 trees and the same squared-error target, with the point forecast and demand head unchanged. All thresholds were selected on calibration A/B; all three heads, including the unchanged shared baseline, were reported.

On the later development block, absolute-error-head MSE was 2.3554 for the shared head, 2.4232 for the 21-feature specialist, and 2.4399 for the 29-feature specialist. The corresponding positive-demand-day MSE values were 7.8403, 7.9048, and 7.9392. No head admitted a common A/B threshold satisfying WAPE at most 0.6401298327 and row coverage at least 35%. Even the separate, outcome-informed largest-prefix diagnostic on the later development block found no feasible prefix at that floor. The policy outputs are therefore empty, with zero row/demand coverage and undefined WAPE.

These are unsuccessful prescription attempts, not a restored operating guarantee. Category specialization changes the training population relative to the shared head; the comparison between specialists holds that population fixed and tests only the added features. Later-block MSE did not improve, although the 29-feature specialist slightly improved calibration-A MSE (2.7195 versus 2.7284 shared). A single-seed intervention cannot identify whether reduced training diversity, estimation variance, or time variation caused the later degradation. The result establishes only that these two changes alone did not resolve the error-information bottleneck. Both interventions still require benchmark pre-mechanical-censoring labels for training.

Verification passed for nine block/method records, all nine optional prediction-array checks, and all three unchanged baseline-head MSE/bias replays. The three retained point forecasts and truth arrays were reconstructed bitwise identically. Added-feature tests checked future perturbations, a deliberately changed origin observation, zero-history conventions, and 18 direct window calculations. Original model hashes are unchanged; no guardian or external input was read.
